How V6 works

Each ship gets 2 actions per turn. Move, Laser, EMP and Hyperspace each cost 1 AP. EMP disables a target for 2 turns and cannot be applied again while that target is disabled. Large Ships have a long-range single-target EMP special. Tiny Kill-Ships do not carry EMPs; their special is Plasma Jump. Singleplayer uses a tactical AI; Local Multiplayer lets two humans alternate turns on the same computer.
